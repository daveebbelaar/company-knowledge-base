Full-time colleagues have 25 paid leave days per year in this fictional policy. Local contracts and public holidays can differ, so People confirms each colleague's balance.

## Planned leave

Request time off in the People calendar at least two weeks ahead when possible. Your manager checks coverage and approves the request. For leave longer than two weeks, discuss the dates before making travel plans.

Before leaving, name a cover person, link active work, and record any deadlines. An out-of-office message should tell people who can help while you are away.

## When you are unwell

Tell your manager as early as reasonably possible. You do not need to describe medical details in a team channel. People will explain any reporting requirements privately.

## Emergencies

Contact your manager or People when an unexpected family situation needs time away. Sort out immediate care first and agree on the handover when you can.
