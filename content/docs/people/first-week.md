Your manager and buddy own your first-week plan. You are not expected to learn the entire company on day one.

## Day one

Meet your buddy, test your work laptop, and enable the password manager and multi-factor authentication. Confirm access to team chat, calendar, and the handbook. Report missing access to your manager rather than borrowing someone else's account.

## By Wednesday

Join a customer walkthrough to see how Fieldbook is used at a maintenance site. Read your department's starting pages. Ask your buddy to explain one decision that is not obvious from the documentation.

## By Friday

Ship one small contribution with your team. It might be a documentation fix, a test, a customer reply draft, or a clearer sales note. Meet your manager to discuss what is still unclear.

## Set up your routine

Read [remote work](/docs/people/remote-work) and [time off](/docs/people/time-off). Ask questions in shared channels so the next new colleague can find the answer.
