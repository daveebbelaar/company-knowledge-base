We work remotely across Europe with a shared overlap window from 10:00 to 14:00 UTC. Keep your calendar current and agree on exceptions with your manager.

## Working from another country

Ask People at least ten working days before temporarily working from a country outside your contracted work location. Include the country and dates. The demo policy allows up to 20 working days per calendar year after approval; local employment, tax, and security restrictions may change what is possible.

A holiday address is not automatically an approved work location. Do not book non-refundable travel around assumed approval.

## Your home office

New starters have a one-time EUR 500 equipment budget for a desk, chair, monitor, or peripherals. Get manager approval and submit receipts through Operations. The company supplies the laptop separately.

## Availability

Use team chat for questions that can wait. Call the on-call engineer for an incident. Outside agreed hours, colleagues are not expected to monitor ordinary messages.

For a missing company device, follow [lost or stolen device](/docs/operations/lost-device).
