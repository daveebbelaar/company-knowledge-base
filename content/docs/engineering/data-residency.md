Fieldbook hosts production customer records and backups in EU regions. The primary region is Frankfurt, with recovery copies in Dublin. Both regions stay inside the European Union.

## What stays in the EU

Work orders, technician assignments, attachments, and service histories remain in these regions. Backups are encrypted and retained for 30 days. Production data is never copied into a developer laptop or a demo environment.

## Questions from a prospect

Sales may state that Fieldbook customer records and backups are hosted in the EU. Requests for country-specific storage, an on-premises installation, or a dedicated single-tenant environment need Engineering review before a commitment.

Our support and billing providers are assessed separately. An EU hosting statement is not a promise that every supplier processes all business information exclusively in the EU.

## Requests to remove data

Route account closure and erasure requests through [data requests](/docs/support/data-requests). Verify the requester before exporting or deleting anything.
