A customer mentioning a good result in a call is not permission to publish their name, logo, or numbers.

## Before drafting

Ask the account owner to confirm that the customer is willing to participate. Record the original source for every number. For a statement such as “planning time fell by 30%,” keep the before and after periods and the customer's measurement method.

## Approval

Send the final wording, named quotes, screenshots, and logo treatment to the customer's designated approver. Written approval must cover the channels where we plan to use the story. Save that approval with the campaign record.

Use an anonymized example if permission is missing. Remove site names, staff names, and account identifiers from screenshots.

## After publication

Recheck older claims every six months. If the customer withdraws permission, pause the material and ask Marketing to track where it appeared.

Follow [launch communication](/docs/marketing/launch-communication) for product announcements.
