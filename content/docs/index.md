Vellumridge Technology builds **Fieldbook**, scheduling and service software for teams that maintain solar parks and wind farms. Our 10 colleagues work across six departments, with a small home base in Utrecht and teammates across Europe.

Everything here describes a fictional company. The policies are examples for this project.

## What we do

A site operator uses Fieldbook to assign technicians, track maintenance visits, and keep service records together. Our customers care about getting a crew to the right place with the right information, even when connectivity is poor.

Our current priority is the **Northstar release**, which improves offline job checklists. Engineering owns delivery. Marketing owns the release story. Sales and Support set expectations using the same release notes.
