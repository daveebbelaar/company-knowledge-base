Report a missing work device immediately, even if you think it may turn up. Do not wait until the next morning.

## First actions

1. Contact the on-call engineer from another device and post in #incidents if you can.
2. Give the device type, last known location, approximate time, and whether it was unlocked.
3. Ask Engineering to revoke active sessions and start the managed-device lock or wipe procedure.
4. Change the credentials Engineering identifies as exposed.

Operations records the incident and arranges a temporary replacement. If it was stolen, get a police report when safe to do so and share the reference with Operations.

## Customer information

Tell Engineering if customer records or credentials may have been accessible. Do not make a customer announcement yourself; the incident lead coordinates that response.

If the device turns up, wait for Engineering clearance before using it again. See [incident response](/docs/engineering/incident-response).
