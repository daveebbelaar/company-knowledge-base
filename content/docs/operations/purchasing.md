Check the approved tools list before buying another subscription. We prefer an existing team license when it solves the problem.

## Approval

Your manager can approve a one-time purchase up to EUR 250. Operations approves larger purchases and every recurring subscription. Send the purpose, price including tax, number of users, and cancellation terms.

A tool that will receive customer data also needs Engineering's security review. A low price or a free trial does not remove that requirement.

## AI tools

Use synthetic or sanitized examples while testing a new AI service. Do not upload customer service records, employee files, secrets, or private contract terms to an unapproved tool.

## After approval

Use the company billing details and assign an internal owner. Record the renewal date and where to cancel. Review unused seats each quarter.

Home office equipment follows [remote work](/docs/people/remote-work).
