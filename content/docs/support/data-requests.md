Only a verified account administrator can request a full account export or closure. A message from an unfamiliar address is not enough, even if it names the customer.

## Verify first

Ask the requester to open the request from their signed-in administrator account. Confirm the scope and destination with the account owner. Route the verified ticket to Operations, which tracks the deadline and coordinates Engineering.

Use the approved secure download flow for exports. Never attach a customer dataset to an ordinary support email or paste it into an AI tool.

## Explain the process

Active records are removed after the approved closure workflow. Recovery copies expire through the normal 30-day backup retention cycle. Any retention exception needs Operations review before Support promises a date.

This is a demo workflow, not a legal policy. For hosting locations and backup details, use [data residency](/docs/engineering/data-residency).
