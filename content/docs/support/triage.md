Read the reported impact before assigning priority. A frustrated subject line alone does not make a request urgent.

## Choose a priority

| Situation | First response target |
| --- | --- |
| Many customers cannot submit work | 15 minutes, any time |
| One account is blocked without a workaround | 1 business hour |
| Feature question or usable workaround | 1 business day |

Business hours are Monday through Friday, 08:00 to 17:00 UTC. The on-call engineer handles widespread outages outside that window.

## First reply

Acknowledge the specific problem. Ask for the affected work-order ID, device type, and approximate time. Request sanitized screenshots only. Never ask for a password or API token.

Search the handbook for a known workaround before escalating. Keep one ticket as the customer-facing thread.

## Route the request

Use [escalations](/docs/support/escalations) for engineering help, [data requests](/docs/support/data-requests) for exports and deletion, and [incident response](/docs/engineering/incident-response) for widespread failures.
