A roadmap item is a direction, not a delivery commitment. Sales can explain the customer problem we are working on and whether a capability is available, in a pilot, or being explored.

## A customer needs a date

Record the use case, number of affected users, and commercial deadline. Ask Product and Engineering for a written assessment. Only an approved contractual milestone can be presented as a commitment.

Avoid “coming next month” unless the date has been approved for that customer. If a deal depends on an unshipped feature, flag it in the opportunity before proposal review.

## Northstar example

Offline checklists are in the Northstar release. Offline scheduling is not. Keep that distinction explicit in demos and proposals.

Share the [launch communication](/docs/marketing/launch-communication) note when a customer asks what offline support includes.
