Use the approved price sheet as the starting point for every quote. Discounts apply to the subscription, not implementation work or third-party charges.

## Approval thresholds

| Subscription discount | Approval required |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 10% | Account executive |
| More than 10%, up to 20% | Head of Sales |
| More than 20% | Finance and the managing director |

Record the reason, term, and approver in the opportunity before sending the quote. A verbal agreement in a call is not approval.

## What to offer first

If a prospect needs a lower initial commitment, propose a smaller pilot or a staged rollout. Do not promise a future feature in exchange for signing this month.

Annual prepayment and another promotion cannot be stacked without Finance approval. Custom payment terms also go to Finance, regardless of the discount.

See [roadmap commitments](/docs/sales/roadmap-commitments) for feature promises.
