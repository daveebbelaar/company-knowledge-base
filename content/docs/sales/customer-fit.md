Fieldbook fits renewable energy service teams with 10 to 200 field technicians and recurring maintenance visits. The buyer is usually an operations lead who coordinates crews across several sites.

## A useful discovery call

Ask how visits are assigned today, what technicians do when a connection drops, and where completed service records go. Establish one measurable pilot outcome, such as reducing unassigned visits or speeding up a service report.

A standard pilot lasts four weeks, covers one region, and includes up to 20 technicians. Agree on the decision-maker, success measure, and review date before the pilot starts.

## When to pause

Pause qualification if the customer requires an on-premises installation, guaranteed offline scheduling, or a feature that only exists on the roadmap. Ask Engineering before including a technical exception.

Use [data residency](/docs/engineering/data-residency) for hosting questions and [discount approvals](/docs/sales/discount-approvals) for commercial exceptions.
